The robot and stopwatch cutouts remain separately identifiable CC BY-SA 4.0 photographic adaptations. Other embedded photos keep their own public-domain or attribution-license status. This register does not grant exclusive rights over third-party material, decide that every composite is legally a mere collection, or automatically license the scientific manuscript under CC BY-SA. Confirm treatment of the adapted images with the publisher before signing a copyright form.

The NAO source page offers CC BY-SA 4.0 but also displays historical EXIF usage information identifying Philippe Dureuil / TOMA and an earlier time-limited assignment. Both are retained in the review notes. No private permission response or publisher clearance has been obtained. A focused clarification request is supplied in PERMISSION_REQUESTS.md.
